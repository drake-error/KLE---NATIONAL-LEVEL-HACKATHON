## 1. The Problem & Real-World Statistics

The current emergency response infrastructure in urban areas is fundamentally flawed due to severe traffic congestion and delayed communication. 

* **The "Golden Hour" Crisis:** The World Health Organization (WHO) and trauma experts define the "Golden Hour" as the first 60 minutes after a traumatic injury. Receiving definitive medical care within this window increases survival rates by over 60%.
* **Traffic-Induced Fatalities:** According to reports aligned with the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), roughly 50% of road crash deaths could be averted with rapid care. Shockingly, an estimated **20% to 30% of these preventable deaths occur en-route** to the hospital strictly due to ambulance delays in dense traffic.
* **Urban Delay Metrics:** In major metro cities (like Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi), average ambulance response times often exceed **20–25 minutes** during peak hours—far above the international standard of 8–10 minutes.

## 3. Revenue & Business Model (How We Make Money)

ResQ-Plus is highly scalable with a dual-pronged revenue approach targeting both public infrastructure and individual safety.

### B2G (Business to Government / Hospitals) - *Primary Revenue*
* **API Licensing & Dashboard Access:** We charge City Traffic Police and Municipal Hospitals an annual enterprise licensing fee to integrate our SOS dispatch API into their existing traffic management control rooms.
* **Smart Traffic Routing Subsidy:** Governments can subsidize the platform to automatically clear "Green Corridors" for ambulances, saving the city millions in emergency management costs.

### B2C (Business to Consumer) - *Freemium Model*
* **Free Tier:** Basic SOS functionality (3 emergency contacts, standard email/WhatsApp alerts). Ensures mass adoption and public safety.
* **ResQ-Plus Premium (Subscription):** For a low monthly fee (e.g., ₹99/month), users get:
  * Integration with Smartwatches/Wearables (Auto-fall detection).
  * Direct API hook to private ambulance aggregators (like StanPlus/RedHealth) for guaranteed 10-minute dispatch.
  * Unlimited emergency contacts & international SMS alerts.
